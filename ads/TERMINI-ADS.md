# Termini: 3 launch ads (Instagram Reels + TikTok)
Format: 9:16 (1080x1920), 15–20 s, works without sound (big text), Albanian on screen.
Brand: black #0b0b0c + white + lime #c8f03c only. Font: Geist (bold for headlines). Logo "termini." with the lime dot.
Look: Apple-clean product shots + Mercedes-precise cinematic macro + the fast kinetic type from the Higgsfield refs
(giant words that fill the frame, cards flying in, UI phone on plain background, hard cuts on the beat).
⚠️ Post only on launch day (keep it discreet until then).

Workflow in Higgsfield: start frames with the FREE image models (see skill higgs-free), spend credits only on video
(image-to-video, 5 s clips). Edit + kinetic type in Premiere/AE or Remotion. Fable (other account) can refine prompts.
