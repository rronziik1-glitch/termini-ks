# Launch plan: Termini + Faqja Gati (Opus, 2026-11-12)

## Order
1. **Before code (week 0):** 15 conversations with real businesses in Gjakova/Prishtina (questions below). If 10/15 say "yes, I'd use it", build.
2. **Fable** designs → **Opus** builds the MVP (2–3 weeks) → **Fable** audits.
3. **Pilot:** 20 businesses set up in person, free.
4. **Launch:** reels + self sign-up.

## The 15 conversations: questions (Albanian, natural)
1. "How do customers book an appointment with you now? Phone, Instagram, in person?"
2. "How many calls/messages a day do you get just for appointments?"
3. "How often does someone not show up? What does it cost you?"
4. "Would you use a link where customers book on their own and get a reminder? It's free."
5. "Do you have a website? If not, why not? How much do you think one costs?"
6. "If you saw a finished page of your business, would you pay €100 once to keep it?"
7. "How do you pay for services like this: cash, bank transfer, card?"

## First 20 businesses (pilot)
- **Who:** 8 barbers, 4 beauty salons, 4 dentists/private clinics, 4 guesthouses/restaurants (Gjakova + Prishtina + Rugova/Prizren).
- **Where to find them:** THAT AD's clients first (warm), then barbershops on the main streets, private clinics around the centre, guesthouses on Booking/Google Maps with few reviews.
- **How:** walk in → set up Termini on their phone for free in 10 minutes → show their finished Faqja Gati page on your phone ("this is yours, if you want it") → film a 15-second clip of them using it (permission).
- **Goal:** 20 active, 5 paid Faqja Gati (€100), 3 video testimonials.

## WhatsApp/DM messages (Albanian)
**First message (cold, after a visit or for an IG business):**
> Përshëndetje! Jam Roni nga Gjakova. Kam bërë një vegël falas ku klientët tuaj e rezervojnë terminin vetë, pa ju thirrur në telefon, dhe u vjen kujtesë në WhatsApp. E rregulloj për ju në 10 minuta, pa pagesë. A keni 10 minuta këtë javë?

**Faqja Gati (after the preview):**
> E përgatita faqen e biznesit tuaj, shikojeni këtu 👉 [link]. Nëse ju pëlqen, me 100€ një herë bëhet e juaja përgjithmonë, me butonin e rezervimit brenda. Pagesë me transfer bankar ose Paysera.

**Reminder for a customer (Termini, automatic):**
> Përshëndetje {emri}! Kujtesë: nesër në {ora} keni termin te {biznesi}. Nëse s'vini dot, anulojeni këtu: {link}. Rezervuar përmes Termini.

| 5 | "Bujtina në Rugovë tani del në Google" | Drone/Osmo of Rugova + the page on a phone | Tourism |
| 6 | "Dentisti që i ka të gjitha terminet në telefon" | Calendar screen, smiling dentist | Clinics |
| 7 | "3 stile, cilin do e zgjidhje? 1, 2 apo 3" | 3 templates side by side (engagement format) | Comments |

Format: 9:16, 15–30s, Albanian subtitles, cinematic motion style (Roni). 3 reels/week.

## 30 days after the MVP
| Days | What |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 15 conversations (questions above); fix the MVP according to what you hear |
| 4–7 | Pilot: 10 businesses set up in person; film clips |
| 8–10 | Reels 1, 2, 7; 10 more businesses |
| 11–14 | Collect feedback, fix bugs, 3 testimonials |
| 15–18 | Open self sign-up; reels 3, 4, 8 |
| 19–22 | First Faqja Gati sales (goal 5 × €100); Paysera live |
| 23–26 | Prishtina + Prizren; reels 5, 6, 9 |
| 27–30 | Metrics: active businesses, bookings/week, no-shows ↓, sales; decide on paid extras |

## Metrics that matter
- Active businesses (with ≥1 booking/week) → goal 50 in 60 days.
- Bookings per week across all businesses.
- Faqja Gati: previews → payments (conversion), goal 10–20%.
- Time to sign up (goal < 2 minutes).
