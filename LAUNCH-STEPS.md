11. YOU: Register the SH.P.K. at ARBK (online, or at the municipal business centre).
    - Use a neutral company name (not "Termini"), because the register is public.
    - Activity: computer programming / web portals.
    - You get the business certificate and fiscal number.
    - Documents for an SH.P.K. (official list, arbk.rks-gov.net):
      1. Form A0 (from arbk.rks-gov.net → Formularët, or at the centre)
      2. Company agreement (Marrëveshja e shoqërisë, law 06/L-016 art. 82.1). With one owner it's a short founding decision.
      3. Statute (Statuti, art. 33). ARBK has free templates: "Modelet e statuteve".
      4. Copy of your ID card or passport (bring the original to show)
      5. Consent of the registered representative (Pëlqimi i përfaqësuesit, art. 24.5). That's you.
    - Free (no fee for a new business). Ready in about 3 working days.
    - Where: online at rbk.rks-gov.net / e-Kosova, or in person at the ARBK centre in your municipality (Gjakova).

## If the project stops
- Domain: nobody can take it while you renew (€10/yr). Keep it, sell it, or let it expire.
  Real risks only: forgetting to renew, your Cloudflare account getting hacked (hence 2-step login), using someone else's brand.
- Server: tell businesses 30 days before, export/delete their data, cancel Vercel/Neon.
- Company: it does NOT close by itself. Pay any debts, get tax clearance from ATK, apply at ARBK to dissolve (the accountant does it).
  With an SH.P.K., company debts stay with the company, not your house or car (unless you sign personal guarantees or break the law).
